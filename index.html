PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Arrow Rush

This policy explains how Gillani Tech handles information in the Android game Arrow Rush (package com.gillanigames.arrowrush). It applies to the current offline version, including its campaign, daily puzzles, themes and earned-coin shop.

01 Information handled by the game

Arrow Rush does not ask you to register, sign in or provide personal information. We do not collect or share personal data through the game. It contains no advertising, analytics, social login or real-money purchasing SDK.

The game stores progress, completed levels, best results, earned coins, power-up inventory, theme choices, daily reward records and sound/vibration settings locally using Android app storage. These records are used to restore your game and settings. They are not sent to Gillani Tech.

02 Children and families

Arrow Rush is intended for players including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. There is no chat, user-generated content, public profile, behavioral advertising or paid purchasing.

Parents or guardians may contact us about a privacy concern. Please do not send children's names, photographs, school details or other unnecessary personal information when requesting help.

03 Permissions and offline play

The game uses Android's vibration permission only for optional vibration feedback, controlled in Settings. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or an advertising identifier. Gameplay works offline.

Opening a privacy-policy link or composing a support email uses your browser or email app. Those services have their own privacy policies; opening them is a separate action from playing offline.

04 Retention, deletion and device backups

Local game records remain until you clear the app's storage, uninstall it, or remove them through your device controls. To erase local records, open Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Rush → Storage → Clear storage. This resets progress and earned items and cannot be undone by us.

Android or your device provider may back up and restore app storage according to your device and account settings. Gillani Tech does not operate that backup service or receive those backups. Check your device's backup controls if you do not want local records restored.

05 Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your request and keep it only as needed to handle support, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Please send only information needed to explain the problem. This voluntary correspondence is separate from data collection in the game.

To request deletion of support correspondence, email us using the address below. We may retain information where required by law. The game has no user accounts to delete.

06 This policy website

This page does not use advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or contact forms. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its own Privacy Statement. We do not add tracking tools to this page.

07 Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the game's data practices change. Adding online services, advertising or paid features would require a fresh review of this policy and the game's Play Console disclosures.
