# Arrow Rush

**Find a clear path. Explore colorful worlds.**

Arrow Rush is an offline arrow maze puzzle game by **Gillani Tech**. Follow the arrows, clear twisting paths and discover 500 unique puzzles across six colorful worlds.

## Your privacy

Arrow Rush requires no account and contains no ads, analytics or real-money purchases. Game progress and settings are stored on your device.

Read our [Privacy Policy](index.html) for details about local saves, children's privacy, optional permissions and support correspondence.
